# Arco 0.4.1

## Recording recovery

- Short pauses in the transcription reader no longer stop native recording after one 100 ms audio tick. A bounded FIFO retains up to 30 seconds of stereo PCM and sends it in order when the reader resumes.
- Audio archiving receives raw PCM directly from the capture clock, before transcription output. Shutdown submits the remaining raw audio even when the transcription pipe has failed.
- Doubao server errors, including `55000000` result timeouts and `55000031` server-busy responses, and `45000081` packet-wait timeouts enter the reconnect path. Unconfirmed audio and new input remain available for ordered replay. Authentication and other request rejections still surface as errors.
- Output writes use nonblocking I/O. Shutdown waits up to two seconds for the PCM queue and then cancels it; the host gives recorder teardown and archive finalization six seconds before forced termination.

## Validation

- Real pipe fault injection: 500 ms reader stall, byte-exact ordered recovery, bounded shutdown with a blocked reader, backlog limit, persistent stall timeout, and closed reader.
- Fake Doubao WebSocket: actual error packets at initialization and during streaming, followed by ordered replay across a new connection while capture continues.
- Core Rust unit and integration tests, recorder self-test, AAC archive round trip, and native package checks.

The PCM queue has a 30-second capacity and a 30-second no-progress timeout. Exceeding either limit still reports a capture error. Live microphone and hour-long provider recovery require a subsequent recording session to verify.
